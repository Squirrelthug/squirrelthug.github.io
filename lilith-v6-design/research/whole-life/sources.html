How far to trust this page. This is AI research agents' synthesis, and most sources were read only as abstracts or summaries. No study was found comparing one integrated assistant with separate apps, so the value of joining everything up is a hypothesis. Quotations were checked by script: those marked not checked could not be tested, and those marked paraphrase were not found in the source they were credited to. Treat the structure and hypotheses as leads and open a source before relying on any claim. What was checked and what failed.

Sources - research wave 2

Every source cited by the five wave 2 tracks, in one list. Ids are the track letter plus the track's own source number (A1 is track A's source 1, B1 is track B's source 1, and so on), so every citation can be traced back to the track file in tracks/. Each entry keeps the reading status the track recorded.

What "read" means in this wave. No track had raw full-text access. The fetch tool returned a model-made digest or condensed version of a page, and the search tool returned tool-written summaries. "Read in part" or "read (condensed)" therefore means "a digest of the page was seen", never a full reading of a paper. No source in wave 2 was read in full in the ordinary sense.

Duplicates across tracks are kept under each track's id so citations stay traceable; cross-references are noted in brackets.

Injection checks. Every track reported that no fetched page contained instructions aimed at the reader.
