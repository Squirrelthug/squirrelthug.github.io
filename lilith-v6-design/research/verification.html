What was checked, and how. Every one of the 154 sources in the bibliography was downloaded by script (PDFs read with pdftotext) and its text cached. Every passage in the findings that is presented in quotation marks and tied to a source tag was then compared with the text of the sources cited next to it. A quote counts as verified only when its words, or at least 85 percent of its five-word sequences, appear in a cited source's own text.

Links

  • Readable, text checked: 85
  • Reachable but little text to check (abstract or landing page): 17
  • Dead (404): 14 (S9, S10, S15, S56, S84, S85, S97, S98, S99, S100, S101, S102, S103, S124). The bibliography marks several of these as read fully. That claim cannot be confirmed.
  • Blocked to scripts (403 or network error): 38. These are mostly publisher and DOI links. They are not necessarily wrong; they are unchecked.

Quotes (315 passages in quotation marks)

  • Verified: 88
  • Could not be checked: 128. A cited source was blocked, so the passage may well be genuine.
  • Not found in the cited source: 53. Every cited source was readable and none contains these words. They are now printed without quotation marks and marked as paraphrase. Most come from two UK civil service pages (S30, S31); S31 is a short page of links and contains none of the sentences attributed to it.
  • Not tied to a source (the research's own proposed wording, or text echoing the brief): 46. Left as written.

What this means for using the research

  • The structure, the questions it raises and the design hypotheses are the agent's synthesis and read as sound leads. They are not established findings.
  • Treat any quotation that carries a "not checked" or "paraphrase" note as unconfirmed until its source has been opened by a person.
  • Checks of the underlying claims (what a study measured, what a statistic was) were not done at scale. Only the quotes and links were. A claim can be wrong even where its quote is verified.
  • The version of the findings published on the site is the corrected one in the verified folder. The original, uncorrected files are kept beside it.
