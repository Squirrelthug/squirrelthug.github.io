🎧 Spoken guide (copy for text to speech) The ten findings

The brief behind this research: Lilith starts as a generic baseline assistant who knows what tools and specialist harnesses exist, helps the user find the gaps in what they ask for, adapts to each person, and moves from getting to know each other, to familiarity, to deeper work. The research asks what experts and history say about doing exactly that.
