## Goal

Lilith's own calendar on top of the Phase-5 mirror: two strictly separated event stores in `calendar_db`; the L1 echo-detection and conflict-flagging job; template L2 agenda summaries; the five scoped tools (two reads, three `lilith_events`-only writes with the s21 guardrails); a local read-only ICS subscription feed with an unguessable token (loopback by default, LAN opt-in); and the `calendar` screen. **No Google Calendar write scope, write method or mutation tool exists anywhere**, the mirror is written only by the §19 sync (plus the single echo annotation column), and cancel never deletes. Excluded: any Google write path, remote feed publishing, other ingest sources (ICS/CalDAV), Lilith managing the user's calendar, maps/address lookup (BD-071), a CWM calendar block (BD-072), onboarding (CH-14).
